Students

This page provides important information and guidance for all students of Atiqah Islamic High School.

Student Guidelines

  • Maintain discipline and uphold Islamic values at all times.
  • Attend all classes punctually and complete assignments on time.
  • Respect teachers, staff, and fellow students.
  • Wear the correct school uniform.

Student Life at Atiqah Islamic High School

Our school provides a balanced environment that nurtures spiritual, academic, and personal growth. Students are encouraged to participate in activities that build discipline, knowledge, and strong character.

  • Daily Qur'an Recitation and Islamic Studies
  • Interactive classroom learning
  • Leadership and responsibility programs
  • Regular mentorship and counseling sessions

Clubs & Student Organizations

Students can join various clubs to explore their talents and passions:

  • Islamic Debate Club
  • Science & Innovation Club
  • Sports Club (Football, Athletics, Netball)
  • Environmental Protection Club
  • Literature & Writers Association

Academic Support Programs

We provide additional support to help students excel academically:

  • Evening revision programs
  • Weekend remedial classes
  • Subject-based discussion groups
  • Access to updated learning materials

Islamic Growth & Discipline

As an Islamic institution, we ensure that our students grow spiritually and morally:

  • Daily Du’a and Dhikr sessions
  • Weekly Islamic seminars
  • Special Halaqah programs
  • Training in proper Islamic etiquette and manners

Important School Rules

To maintain a peaceful and productive environment, students are expected to follow these rules:

  • Respect teachers and fellow students at all times
  • Maintain personal cleanliness and proper uniform
  • Keep the school compound clean
  • Avoid misuse of mobile phones or prohibited items
  • Attend all lessons and school programs punctually
